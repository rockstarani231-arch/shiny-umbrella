ACCOUNTS & ACTIVITY

Stored in this browser

Accounts, profiles, recent checks, notifications, reports, settings, and watchlist entries stay in this browser and do not sync. There is no server-backed account recovery.

◷
USE YOUR JUDGMENT

Checks are not guarantees

SHIELD uses local pattern rules and locally saved reports. It has no AI service or live URL threat feed, and a low-risk result cannot prove something is safe.

ⓘ
Use trusted channels.

Do not reply, click, send money, or share codes when something feels wrong. Contact the organization using details from an official source. Use your device’s own tools to block calls and texts.
