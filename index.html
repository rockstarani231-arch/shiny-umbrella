PRIVACY & SAFETY

Your data stays
your business.

Choose when SHIELD uses a connected service for added URL checks.

⌑
LOCAL BY DEFAULT

Message and file checks stay here

Message, SMS, and file checks run in your browser. URL structure checks are also local unless you connect a server.

↗
OPTIONAL URL LOOKUP

Connect a SHIELD server

When connected, the URL you check is sent to your server and may be checked by Google Safe Browsing, VirusTotal, and OpenAI, depending on which server keys are configured.

✳
HELPFUL, NOT INFALLIBLE

Use your judgment

AI reviews the URL text only; it cannot confirm page contents. Reputation feeds may miss new threats. A low-risk result is not proof of safety.

◷
YOU'RE IN CONTROL

Keep private links private

Do not submit links containing private access tokens or account reset codes. Recent checks and reports are saved in this browser only.

ⓘ
SHIELD will not visit a URL.

URL reputation checks compare the submitted address with configured services. Do not treat a clean result as a guarantee.
